Interactive Aircraft Explorer

This isn't an illustration — it's built from an actual scan of a 777-300ER, so the proportions are the real ones, not an artist's guess. Spin it side-on and look at the engines. Each one is wide enough that a 737's fuselage could almost fit through the intake, and that's usually the moment people realize how genuinely enormous a widebody's engines are next to everything around them. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and click a glowing marker to fly in on a part — start with an engine or the landing gear, both look almost absurd up close.

Every one of the eighteen hotspots connects to something explained in more depth elsewhere on this site — the wing shape to Aerodynamics, the control surfaces to Flight Dynamics, the systems tucked out of sight to Aircraft Systems. If something you clicked surprised you, that's usually a sign there's a whole page here worth reading next.
