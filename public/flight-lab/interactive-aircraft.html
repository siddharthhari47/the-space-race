Interactive Aircraft Explorer

A real 3D model you can rotate freely — drag to spin it around, scroll or pinch to zoom in. It rotates gently on its own; grab it any time to look closer at a wing, an engine, or the tail.

Drag anywhere on the model to rotate it, and scroll or pinch to zoom. Release it and it picks the slow spin back up right where you left it.
