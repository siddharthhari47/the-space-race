Why did that happen? Assumptions and sources

Moving a load changes the balance point without changing total mass. In this model, that can fix the loading check while leaving the ground run exactly as long. Removing cargo changes total mass and can reduce the ground run, but dropping below the required payload fails the mission.

Balance and runway are separate checks. CG is calculated as Σ(mass × station) / total mass. The runway equation receives that exact total mass; it does not calculate the effect of CG on rotation or climb.

This mission reuses the fictional loading envelope and ground-run model from the standalone experiments. Wing area 16 m², ground CL 0.60, CD 0.055, maximum CL 1.60, rolling coefficient 0.025. Thrust is 2,400 N × density / 1.225 kg/m³ at full setting; lift-off is assumed at 1.20 times stall speed with instantaneous rotation. No wind, slope, fuel burn, obstacle clearance, real structural capacities or climb calculation. Station choices for fuel represent alternative tank locations, not in-flight fuel transfer.
