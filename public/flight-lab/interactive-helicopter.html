Interactive Helicopter Explorer

Unlike an aircraft, a helicopter doesn't need to build up speed along a runway to generate lift. Its rotor is doing the work right above your head, the whole time. Rotate the model and click a component to see what it does — the rotor and swashplate first if you're not sure where to start, since between them they explain most of what makes a helicopter behave so differently from anything with fixed wings.

Two of the nine hotspots — the rotor hub and mast, the engine bay — don't have their own separate geometry on this model, so their markers are placed close by rather than exactly on top of a part you could pick out by eye. Everything else lines up with something real you can see on the model. Want the physics behind what you just clicked? Read Helicopter Aerodynamics next.
