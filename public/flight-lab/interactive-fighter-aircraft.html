Interactive Fighter Aircraft Explorer

A 777 spends its whole life trying to be efficient. This aircraft has almost the opposite problem: it has to put an enormous amount of energy exactly where the pilot wants it, right now. Start with the engines at the back, then look at how small the wing is compared to the body. Those two things explain most of why a fighter is shaped nothing like the airliner you've already been through.

Six of the eleven markers sit on parts this model gives as their own separate geometry, so they land exactly on the thing they name: the canopy, the cockpit, the landing gear, the underwing stores. The other five (nose, intakes, engines, wings, tails) sit on one continuous airframe mesh, so they're positioned from the real aircraft's published dimensions instead. That's honest rather than exact, and you'll see an "Estimated placement" badge on those. It works because the model happens to be built at true scale, 1 unit to 1 metre, which was checked against the real Su-35's length, span, and height before any marker was placed.
