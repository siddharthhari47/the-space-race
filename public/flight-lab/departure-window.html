Why did that happen? Sources and limits

NPTEL Flight lecture 62, PDF pages 2–5, relates ground run to density and accelerating force. In this fictional model, temperature changes density at the fixed pressure altitude. Density then affects the assumed thrust and lift-off speed, as well as lift and drag during the run.

We reuse the existing runway equations unchanged. CG is a separate loading check, not an input to runway distance. No climb, obstacles, wind, slope, engine temperature limits or real aircraft performance is calculated. The comparison ends at the model's lift-off condition. Air temperatures are game settings.
