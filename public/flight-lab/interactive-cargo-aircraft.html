Interactive Cargo Aircraft Explorer

Here's a question worth holding onto while you rotate this one. Why does a freighter look so obviously different from an airliner, when both are just tubes with wings? Start with where the wing meets the body, then look at the tail from the side. Neither of those is a styling decision. They both come from the same thing: this aircraft was designed around a box, and the airliner was designed around people.

Seven of the nine markers sit on parts this model gives as their own geometry, including all four propeller discs, the full-span wing, both stabilizers, and the landing gear structures. The flight deck and rear ramp don't have separate meshes, so those two are positioned from where they have to be on an aircraft laid out like this, and carry an "Estimated placement" badge to say so.

One thing this page deliberately doesn't do is tell you which aircraft this is. It's a stylised model rather than a replica of a specific type, and its proportions don't match any freighter in service closely enough to put a name on it. So everything here explains how this class of aircraft works, without quoting performance figures that would belong to some other, real aeroplane.
