The Space Race

Scroll to launch.

A rocket is a sequence of decisions. Move through the opening and watch the vehicle trade a quiet pad for a dark sky.

Keep scrolling ↓
01 / 05

Everything starts on the pad.

The vehicle is mostly decisions waiting to become motion. For now, the only thing winning is gravity.

02 / 05

Thrust has to win.

The engines wake up before the rocket moves. A launch begins when the force pushing up is greater than the weight pulling down.

03 / 05

Thrust > weight.

The pad starts to fall away. The exhaust gets longer, the camera follows, and the vehicle finally has somewhere to go.

04 / 05

Mass is leaving with the flame.

As propellant burns, the same thrust moves less mass. Acceleration is no longer a promise — it is visible in the climb.

05 / 05

Now the problem changes.

Getting high was only the beginning. Orbit is sideways speed, held long enough to keep missing the ground.
