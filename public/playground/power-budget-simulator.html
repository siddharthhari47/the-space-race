Power Budget Simulator

Every satellite has to generate at least as much power as it uses, averaged over an orbit that includes real time spent in Earth's shadow with the panels producing nothing at all. Adjust the numbers and see if the budget closes.

A budget that barely balances on paper is exactly the margin that fails once batteries degrade or panels get shadowed by the spacecraft's own structure — real missions build in more headroom than the math strictly requires.
